# Retail-Sales-Analysis

This **Excel-based Retail Sales Dashboard** provides a comprehensive analysis of retail business performance through interactive reports and dynamic visualizations. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, Slicers, and Timeline Filters**.

The primary objective of this project is to help business stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), evaluate sales and profitability trends, identify high-performing products and customers, and support data-driven decision-making for business growth.

## Business Insights

### Monthly Sales Trend

The dashboard analyzes month-wise sales performance to identify seasonal trends and revenue fluctuations throughout the year. These insights help businesses forecast demand and optimize sales strategies.

### Category Sales Analysis

Sales and profit are compared across three major product categories:

* Furniture
* Office Supplies
* Technology

This analysis helps identify the highest revenue-generating and most profitable business segments.

### Ship Mode Performance

The dashboard evaluates sales generated through different shipping methods, including:

* First Class
* Same Day
* Second Class
* Standard Class

This enables businesses to understand customer shipping preferences and improve logistics planning.

### Profit by Sub-Category

Profitability is analyzed across multiple product sub-categories such as:

* Storage
* Laptops
* Phones
* Bookcases
* Tables
* Binders
* Accessories
* Paper
* Chairs

This helps identify high-profit and low-profit products for better inventory and pricing decisions.

### Regional Sales Analysis

The dashboard compares sales performance across different regions:

* East
* North
* South
* West

Regional insights enable businesses to identify strong-performing markets and areas requiring improvement.

### Top Customers Analysis

The dashboard highlights the **Top 10 Customers** based on total sales, helping businesses recognize valuable customers and improve customer relationship strategies.

### Top Products Analysis

The dashboard identifies the **Top 10 Best-Selling Products**, enabling businesses to focus on products that contribute the most to overall revenue.

## Project Outcome

This dashboard demonstrates how **Microsoft Excel** can be used as a powerful **Business Intelligence (BI)** tool to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights. By consolidating sales, profit, customer, product, and regional performance into a single interactive dashboard, it enables faster reporting, effective performance monitoring, and informed business decision-making.
